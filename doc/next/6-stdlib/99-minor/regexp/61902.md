New [Regexp.All], [Regexp.AllIndex], [Regexp.AllString], [Regexp.AllStringIndex],
[Regexp.AllStringIndex], [Regexp.AllStringSubmatchIndex], [Regexp.AllSubmatch], and
[Regexp.AllSubmatchIndex] iterator methods on [Regexp] allow incremental matching of the input.

The new [Regexp.SplitSeq] method iterates over the results of splitting the input with the
regular expression.
